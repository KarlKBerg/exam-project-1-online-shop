# How I used AI for this project

## Design

For the design part of the assignment, I used AI as a helper for finding inspiration. I used the prompt: _I am designing an e-commerce website for my exam. Later I will code the website. I want you to assist me in finding inspiration, generate text for newsletter, legal pages etc. You are not to give me any answers or inputs that will change the layout, colors or design without me directly asking for it._

## Coding

For the coding part of the assigment, I used AI as a "teacher". By this, I mean that I did not ask for how to do any of the code, but rather have the AI explain what I was struggeling with. By using the AI like this, I got a better and broader understanding of code that I had a hard time figuring out.
